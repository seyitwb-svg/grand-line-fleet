Monetize an API without Stripe: crypto pay-per-call, no KYC

Stripe needs a business entity, bank account, KYC documents, and weeks of onboarding. If you're selling to developers — or to AI agents — there's a lighter stack: per-call USDC payments on Base, verified on-chain, settled straight to your wallet in seconds.

The two viable rails

x402 (HTTP 402, USDC on Base) — the agent-native one. Your endpoint answers 402 with payment requirements; the caller pays on-chain and retries with the tx hash. No accounts, no API keys, no checkout page. Coinbase's Bazaar indexes facilitator-served resources; facilitator-free serving works too and costs you nothing but an RPC call.

Plain crypto checkout (BTC/USDT) — the human-native one. A unique jittered amount per order lets you auto-match payments to orders with nothing but mempool.space/trongrid API calls. ~200 lines total.

What you skip

  • No merchant account, no PCI scope, no chargeback handling
  • No card data, no 3DS flows, no currency conversion dance
  • No custodial processor holding your money hostage

What you trade

  • Buyers need crypto (USDC on Base is the cheapest path — sub-cent fees)
  • No invoicing/tax-doc automation — you handle accounting yourself
  • Refunds are manual (you send the tx yourself)

The honest fit test

x402 pay-per-call shines for: API calls under $1, agent-consumable resources (renders, checks, transforms), and sellers without a business entity. It's wrong for: enterprise customers needing invoices, anything regulated, subscriptions with complex billing.
