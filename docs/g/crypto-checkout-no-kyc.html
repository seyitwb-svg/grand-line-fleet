Paying for software in BTC — how our checkout works

We sell self-hosted source. Self-hosters shouldn't need to hand identity documents to a payment processor to buy code.

The mechanics

  1. You click buy - we quote an exact satoshi amount (plus a tiny unique jitter) and a BTC address — or a USDT-TRC20 amount for stablecoin payers.
  2. You send the transfer from any wallet or exchange.
  3. We watch the mempool via public APIs; the unique amount identifies your order. One network confirmation - your download link unlocks.

No account, no email verification, no card. The payment itself is the receipt — your txid is the proof of purchase.

Why it matters for liability

There is no merchant account to freeze, no platform to deplatform either side, and no personal data to leak — we store an order id, an amount, and a txid.
