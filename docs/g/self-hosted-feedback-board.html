Self-hosted Canny alternative

Canny starts at $79/month for a feedback board: a table of ideas, a vote button, a changelog. For a small team that is nearly $1,000 a year — for a CRUD app.

What self-hosting gets you

  • Your data stays yours. Feature requests are product strategy — keep them on your own disk.
  • Flat cost. Heard runs on ~50 MB of RAM. Any $4/month VPS fits it next to everything else you host.
  • No per-seat pricing. Unlimited admins, unlimited boards.

What Heard ships with

Public idea boards, voting without accounts, moderation queue, status labels (planned / shipped — shipping auto-posts a changelog entry), changelog RSS, and a 5-minute Docker deploy.

The honest trade-off

You run upgrades and backups yourself (a docker pull and a cron cp). You lose Canny's integrations — if your workflow lives inside Intercom, self-hosted is the wrong choice.
